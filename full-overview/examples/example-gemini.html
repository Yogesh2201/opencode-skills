01 What the company does

One monopoly wearing a four-vertical costume. IRCTC sits between Indian Railways and the passenger — fee on booking, margin on meal, package on tour, contract on water.

The idea in one line

The business is a legislated tollbooth + operating-margin engine on the same passenger base.

Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation (Navratna Cat-1) is the sole authorised digital gateway — IRCTC Rail Connect ticketing, catering + e-catering + Vande Bharat meals, Bharat Gaurav / Maharajas' Express tourism, and Rail Neer water plants. Two engines: regulated fee engine (stable, government-priced convenience fee) and operating-margin engine (catering, tourism, water — pricing power). Over 3Y they diverged — revenue +15% but PAT only +6% on OPM 36% → 31% compression. Conflating the four verticals is the single most common mistake.
