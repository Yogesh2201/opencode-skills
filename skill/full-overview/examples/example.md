# IRCTC — Full Overview (Example MD)

**NSE: IRCTC · BSE: 532777 · New Delhi · Navratna PSU · Filings-only · 03 Sep 2026 · Price ₹473 (Sep 2 close)**

## Core idea

IRCTC doesn't sell tickets — it sells **the right to monetise 22 million daily passengers four times**: a regulated **fee engine** (convenience fee — stable, government-priced) plus an **operating-margin engine** (catering, tourism, Rail Neer — pricing power). Over 3Y they diverged — revenue **+15%** but PAT only **+6%** on **OPM 36% → 31%** compression. Conflating the four verticals is the single most common mistake.

## 01 — What the company does

**One line:** a legislated tollbooth + operating-margin engine on the same passenger base.

**Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation (Navratna Cat-1)** is the **sole authorised digital gateway** — Rail Connect ticketing, catering + e-catering + Vande Bharat meals, Bharat Gaurav / Maharajas' Express tourism, Rail Neer water plants.

- **Legislated monopoly** — sole licensee, 4 verticals; barrier is a Cabinet note, not capital.
- **Two-speed model** — fee capped, margin volatile; dispersion lives in catering + Rail Neer. OPM 31% is the floor debate.
- **Cash machine** — FCF ₹1,227 Cr on NP ₹1,393 Cr · D/E 0.02 · payout 52% every year post-IPO.

**Value flow:** Passengers (~22M daily) → IRCTC Tollbooth (fee · meal · tour · water, sole license) → Monetization (fee · catering · tourism · Rail Neer).

## 05 — Trends, big messages, flags, verdict

**Five trends:** (1) OPM floor — 31% floor, 33–34% bull; +15% rev vs +6% PAT. (2) Fee-cap binary — ~28.8% gated by Ministry. (3) Rail Connect flywheel — ~22M daily, zero fuel elasticity. (4) Vande Bharat + tourism — meals annuity, 9.6% with operating leverage. (5) Overhang + yield — −34% 1Y; 52% payout + net cash cushion; Govt 62.40% caps re-rating.

**Big messages:** Growth — sole gateway, monetise one passenger 4× · Positioning — fee stable, margin 31% → 33–34% · Capability — FCF ₹1,227 Cr · net cash ₹3,419 Cr · payout 52%.
