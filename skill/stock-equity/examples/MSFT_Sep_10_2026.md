## 1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

*Microsoft Corporation* is the **default operating layer of global enterprise IT** — the M365 productivity annuity, the Azure cloud platform, the Windows/Gaming consumer franchise, and the Copilot AI stack stitched across all three. A `$3.93T` company carrying an `AAA` credit rating (one of only two US corporates), Microsoft sits at the center of the largest private infrastructure build-out in corporate history.

- **One company wearing three P&Ls.** Behind the single ticker sit three distinct engines: the *renewal annuity* (M365 commercial, Windows Server — recurring, price-raising, ~`70%` gross margin), the *growth engine* (Azure, `+31%` FY26), and the *option portfolio* (Copilot, Gaming, OpenAI stake). Conflating them is the single most common mistake on this name.
- **Mental model:** when you read any MSFT number, ask whether it describes the *installed-base annuity* (stable, compounding at `+12-15%`) or the *AI infrastructure bet* (`$88.7B` cash capex, growing `+35%+`). The two have opposite effects on free cash flow — and FY26 is the first year the second engine visibly bent the first's curve.
- **Why the two-engine framing matters here:** it explains why revenue grew `+15.4%` (3Y CAGR) while FCF grew only `+10.6%` over the same period — the annuity funds the build-out, and the market is now pricing whether the build-out pays.
