## Core idea

TD Power Systems doesn't sell generators off a shelf — it sells **certainty of power where failure is not an option**. Model: **engineering hours + precision manufacturing under one roof**, monetized through **~45 global OEM partnerships**. FY25 breakout: **+41% order inflow, 68% export** — gas power + data-centre demand met TDPS's sweet spot (1–45 MVA custom niche). Q1 FY27: inflow ₹734 Cr (+87% YoY), **93% export**.

## 01 · What the company does

Custom-engineered AC generators (**1–250 MVA**) + motors, spec'd per prime mover (steam, gas, hydro, wind, diesel, gas engine), site, and grid code. Customers = turbine/engine OEMs. Revenue = project sales + 20–30 yr services/spares/refurbishment. 3 plants, one roof, Dabaspet.

**Where money comes from** (order/installed proxies — revenue by region not disclosed):
- Q1 FY27 inflow ₹734 Cr → 93% export / 7% domestic
- FY26 inflow ₹2,238 Cr → ~79% export / ~21% domestic
- Order book ₹2,208 Cr → 69.5% exports / 17.9% domestic / 9.6% railway
- Q1 export ₹257→₹684 Cr (2.7×); domestic ₹135→₹51 Cr. FY25 mix 51/49 → FY26 mix 16/84 (India/export).

## 03 · Key products

- Steam turbine generators to 250 MVA (67.5 MVA Maxau, largest 4-pole)
- Gas turbine generators to 250 MVA (8× 9.1 MW, 1800 rpm, US OEM)
- Hydro to 45 MVA (custom per head/grid)
- Wind: PMG / synchronous / DFIG
- Gas engine to 25 MVA (500+ to a major engine OEM; J612/J624/J920)
- Diesel to 25 MVA (9× 8.586 MVA UAE barge)
- Induction motors, synchronous + traction, special apps (geothermal, solar thermal, oil & gas)
- Order-book mix (31 Mar 2026, ₹19.73 bn): generators 85% · rail 12.5%.

## 05 · Trends & big messages

1. **Data-centre & gas surge** — market $51.99B→$72.66B (2029); 100 GW US spike by 2030 → 40–45 MW generators.
2. **Hybrid transition** — wind *and* gas *and* hydro; hydro stable + PMG/DFIG + gas bridge.
3. **Export supercycle** — ₹14,783M inflow (+41%), 68% export; drives FY27–28.
4. **Capacity ahead of revenue** — UK centre + 3rd plant (Q4 FY26); FY25 EBITDA ₹2,544M (+38.6%), PAT ₹1,746M (+47.5%).
5. **Volatility** — global growth 2.8% vs 3.3%; custom engineering as hedge.
- Guidance ratchet: ₹2,000 → ₹2,200 → ₹2,400 → ₹2,600 Cr. Roadmap: FY26 ₹1,856 → FY27 ₹2,600 → FY28 ~₹3,200 → FY29–30 >₹4,000 (capacity, not forecast).
- Watch: export inflow STRONG · gas-engine orders GROWING · book ₹2,208 Cr · utilisation WATCH · ASP/MW RISING · concentration RISK.
