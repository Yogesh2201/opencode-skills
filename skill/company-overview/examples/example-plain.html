Company Profile · Engineered-to-Order Power

TD Power Systems — Custom-Built Power for a Global Niche

India's engineered-to-order AC generator pioneer — 1 to 250 MVA for every prime mover, 111 countries, 7,200+ installations. Not a catalog box-shipper. A 26-year project OEM that engineers the machine to the turbine, then services it for 20 years.

The core idea. TD Power Systems doesn't sell generators off a shelf — it sells certainty of power where failure is not an option. Its model is engineering hours + precision manufacturing under one roof monetized through ~45 global OEM partnerships. The last year's breakout — +41% order inflow, 68% export — happened because gas power and data-centre demand finally met TDPS's sweet spot: the 1–45 MVA custom niche that giants find too tailored to bother with. Q1 FY27 update: inflow ₹734 Cr (+87% YoY) was 93% export — the flip is accelerating.

01 What the company does

Engineered-to-order, not catalog — one sentence, then the mechanism (plus where the money actually comes from).

One line: engineering hours + metal

TDPS custom-engineers the generator to the turbine/engine and the site — steam, gas, hydro, wind, diesel, gas — plus the service life that follows.

TD Power Systems designs and manufactures custom-built AC generators and motors where every machine is spec'd to the prime mover, site altitude, temperature, and grid code. Customers are the ~45 global OEMs who build the prime mover; TDPS is their generator specialist inside the package. Revenue is project sales plus services, spares, and refurbishment over a 20–30 year life. [AR FY25 Corporate Overview; tdps.co.in/generators]

05 Key trends & big messages

What AR FY25 and BSE filings say is moving the market — and what management repeats in every filing. Filings-only.

Five trends the filings name — and the management line on each

Each card is one filed trend + the big message underneath. Sources: AR FY25 MD letter + "Sector in Sync" + BSE Jan 29 2026 outlook.
↗ Trend 1 · demand supercycle

Data centre & gas turbine surge

Global generator market $51.99B (2025) → $72.66B (2029). AI farms + fracking + Ukraine rebuild. Data centre alone → 100 GW US spike by 2030. → TDPS: 40–45 MW generators, deliveries FY26.

⚡ Trend 2 · hybrid transition

Energy transition = "and", not "or"

Not wind or gas — wind and gas and hydro. Grids need dispatchable + renewable. TDPS portfolio hits all three. → Hydro stable + wind PMG/DFIG + gas bridge

✦ Trend 3 · export supercycle

Export is the growth engine

Highest-ever inflow ₹14,783M (+41% YoY) in FY25 — 68% exports/deemed exports. Q1 FY27 hit 93%. "Will drive FY27 & FY28." → 45 OEMs · healthy order book into FY26

⚙ Trend 4 · capacity bet

Engineering & capacity ahead of revenue

UK Design Centre + third plant (ramp Q4 FY26) + motors. FY25 EBITDA ₹2,544M (+38.6%), PAT ₹1,746M (+47.5%) on mix + leverage. → Capex precedes margin — watch Q4 ramp

⚠ Trend 5 · volatility demands adaptability

Global growth easing to 2.8% (2025) vs 3.3% (2024); demand uneven — India offers scale/stability/opportunity. TDPS line: "Solutions that function across complex environments and adapt to changing needs, without pause or compromise."
