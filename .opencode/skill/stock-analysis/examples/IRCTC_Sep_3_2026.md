## 1. COMPANY OVERVIEW
**Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation Ltd (IRCTC)** is the **sole authorised digital gateway** to Indian Railways — running the catering monopoly, the IRCTC Rail Connect / Tourism portals, Bharat Gaurav and Maharajas' Express rail tourism, and the Rail Neer packaged-water plant network. A `Navratna (Category 1)` PSU under the Ministry of Railways, the company effectively sits on a **legislated tollbooth** between `~22 million` daily rail passengers and `4` parallel monopoly verticals.

- **One-monopoly company wearing a "single product" costume.** Behind the `4` regulated verticals sits a single underlying engine — the Indian Railways passenger base — that monetises through a per-booking convenience fee, a per-meal catering margin, a per-passenger tourism package, and a per-bottle water contract. Conflating the four verticals is the single most common mistake on this stock.
- **Mental model:** when you read any IRCTC number, ask whether it describes the *regulated fee engine* (stable, government-priced) or the *operating-margin engine* (catering, tourism, water — where IRCTC has pricing power). The two have moved in opposite directions over the last `3Y`.
- **Why the two-speed framing matters here:** it explains why revenue grew `+15%` TTM and PAT grew only `+6%` — the fee engine is regulated and capped; the margin engine is where the dispersion lives. The market has read this `OPM` compression (`36% → 31%`) as terminal-multiple erosion and de-rated the stock `−34%` in `1Y`.
