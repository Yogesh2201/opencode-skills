🧠 Core idea
TD Power Systems doesn't sell generators off a shelf — it sells certainty of power where failure is not an option. Its model is engineering hours + precision manufacturing under one roof monetized through ~45 global OEM partnerships. The last year's breakout — +41% order inflow, 68% export — happened because gas power and data-centre demand finally met TDPS's sweet spot: the 1–45 MVA custom niche that giants find too tailored to bother with.

🔄 What changed
Before: TDPS operated as a conventional generator seller serving catalog demand across all sectors. After: Gas-power and data-centre demand aligned with TDPS's 1–45 MVA custom niche, creating a structural shift. Q1 FY27 update: inflow ₹734 Cr (+87% YoY) was 93% export — the flip is accelerating, signaling the niche is becoming the primary revenue engine.

💰 Why it works
- Revenue TTM ₹2,124 Cr (FY26 ₹1,856 Cr, +27.8% YoY); Q1 FY27 ₹640 Cr (+71% YoY)
- Operating profit ₹383 Cr TTM on 18.0% OPM; ROCE 34.0%, ROE 24.7%; almost debt-free
- Order book ₹2,208 Cr at 30 Jun 2026 — 3.4× coverage on FY26 revenue; 93% export inflow Q1 FY27
- Highest-ever inflow ₹14,783M (+41% YoY) in FY25, 68% exports/deemed; 45 OEMs · 111 countries
- FY27 guidance ratcheted from ₹2,000 Cr → ₹2,600 Cr (+30%) across four earnings calls

👀 Hidden insight
The real story is not "scale versus disruption" but "specialization as moat": TDPS's relevance comes from engineering depth (custom rotor/stator per prime mover) and an under-the-radar export franchise, not from competing with giant catalog players. The orange "68% export" hinge appearing twice in the document — once as a gauge and once as the closing band — reveals that export is the single leverage point that compounds or undermines every other trend.

⚠️ Open question
Can TDPS maintain the export growth trajectory while scaling capacity? The "value-chain-move-up" principle that worked when giants ceded the 1–45 MVA niche may not hold if OEMs vertically integrate — or if fiscal/geopolitical shifts curb export demand. The compounding question is whether human judgment can stay ahead of automation in this custom niche.

🎯 One-line takeaway
TD Power Systems doesn't sell generators — it sells certainty of power where failure is not an option, and the next chapter is US data-centre gas power at 40–45 MW + hydro stability + export OEM depth — engineered in Bengaluru, designed in the UK, installed in 111 countries.
